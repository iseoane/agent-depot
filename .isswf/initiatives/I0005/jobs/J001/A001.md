## Root cause (hypothesis in the brief REFUTED)

- The recipe `reload()` triggered by Esc (`src/tui/sources-recipes.tsx:212`, also `:269`, `:297`) is read-only: `apps.load()` is `readdir` + `readFile`, and `approvalStatus()` is `readFile`. It writes nothing, and its setState calls are already guarded by `mounted.current`. It cannot cause ENOTEMPTY.
- The actual writer is the Catalog view. The failing test's last step is `view.stdin.write("2")` followed by a wait for `/▶ \[2\] Catalog/`. Mounting Catalog starts a chain of source-state reads: `listSources`, `discoverSkills`, `listUserGlobalInstallations`, and the installed-skills scan. Every one goes through `SourceStateStore.acquireLock()` (`src/source-state.ts:252`), which does `mkdir -p state/` and `mkdir state/sources.json.lock`, even for `load()`.
- The test returns as soon as the Catalog header appears, still inside "Loading catalog…". `t.after` then runs `rm(home, {recursive})` while those lock mkdirs are in flight, so `rmdir state` fails with ENOTEMPTY.

Evidence:
1. Reproduced under CPU load (24 parallel runs of the single test, `dist/tests/tui-sources-recipes.test.js`): 13/72 and 18/72 failed with exactly the CI error (`ENOTEMPTY ... rmdir '/tmp/sources-recipes-XXXX/state'`). Sequential runs never failed (20/20), so the sequential 20-run acceptance does not discriminate the bug.
2. A temporary `readdir` in the dist teardown showed the leftover entry `state/sources.json.lock`. A `[]` result meant the lock was released before the listing.
3. A stack trace in `acquireLock` (temporary, dist only) showed the callers: `sources-view` (startup), then `catalog-state` `useCatalogSkills` (`listSources`, `discoverSkills`, `selectSources`, `packages.discover`) and `loadInstalledSkills` (`listUserGlobalInstallations`, `listSources`, `loadPackageOwnedSkillFilter`).
4. Discriminating control: removing only the final `"2"` and Catalog wait (keeping the Esc reload) gave 0/96 failures, all 96 runs passed. Keeping `"2"` gave 13-18/72.

## Fix and why test-level

The test now waits for `▶ [2] Catalog` without `Loading catalog` (`tests/tui-sources-recipes.test.tsx`, last step of the test). Catalog's `loading` flag (`catalog-view.tsx:118`) covers skills discovery, app catalog and installed-skills load, so nothing is in flight at teardown.

No product change. The in-flight work is `SourceOperations` filesystem calls. An unmounted view can only ignore their results, which `useCatalogSkills` already does via `cancelled`. Actually cancelling them would need abort support through `SourceStateStore` and the operations API, which is out of scope. The recipe code needs no change because its reload only reads and is guarded by `mounted`.

Rejected alternatives:
- `rm` with `maxRetries`: hides the race, and a late `acquireLock` can recreate `home/state` after `rm`, leaking a temp dir.
- Proxy-tracking operations promises in the fixture: tried. It missed the Package operations' own `packages.json.lock` under `home/.local/state`, which moved the failure to `rmdir home`. It was reverted as fragile and incomplete.
