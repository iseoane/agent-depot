## Likely product bug (report, not skipped)
`src/process-runner.ts` itself says an argument containing `"` can reach the program altered through an npm shim's `%*` re-parse. So `say "hi"` and `a\\"b` are the most likely failures of the new round-trip test. `%PATH%` is also uncertain: the `^%` escape on a `/c` command line may still allow expansion. If these fail on windows-latest, that is a product finding for #41/#42, not a test fault. Do not skip them.
